Why we're doing this.

This questionnaire is part of the Discovery Phase for Beyond the Classroom. Before we develop a proposal or recommend a way forward, we want to understand the project as a whole: what you hope to build around the book, where you see yourself going, who you want to reach, what is already in motion, and where you see Ace Pen Studio fitting into that picture.

There are no right or wrong answers here, and you do not need to have everything figured out. The purpose is to give us enough context to think with you about the project's direction, structure and possibilities.

If you have general questions about publishing or how publishing works, you can find answers in the Ace Pen Studio FAQs.
